You’re all set

  1. When Stop Yapping asks for microphone access, click Allow.
  2. In Zoom, Meet, Discord, Teams and your other call apps, choose Stop Yapping Mic as the microphone.
  3. Keep your regular microphone in Vowen, with its local CLI integration turned on.

Stop Yapping lives in the menu bar. From its icon you can pause muting, pick a microphone, open it at login or uninstall it.
