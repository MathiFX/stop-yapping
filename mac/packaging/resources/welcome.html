Stop Yapping mutes your call mic while you dictate with Vowen.

This installs two things:

  • Stop Yapping, a small app in your menu bar.
  • Stop Yapping Mic, a virtual microphone for your call apps. It is built from BlackHole by Existential Audio (GPL-3.0).

macOS will ask for your password because the virtual microphone is a system audio driver. No restart is needed.
