Stop Yapping makes your call mic go quiet while you dictate with Vowen.

This installer includes the original VB-CABLE driver by VB-Audio. If it is missing, its installer will open and Windows will ask permission to install the driver. A restart may be needed.

VB-CABLE is donationware. If you find it useful, you can support VB-Audio at https://vb-audio.com/Cable/.

After setup, choose CABLE Output as the microphone in the calling apps you want to mute. Keep your regular microphone selected in Vowen.
